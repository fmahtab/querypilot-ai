# RetailStar — Company Overview

## Who We Are

RetailStar is a specialty art and craft retailer headquartered in the United States. We serve makers at every level — from professional artists working in oil and acrylic to first-time crafters picking up a glue gun or skein of yarn for a weekend project.

What began as a neighborhood craft shop has grown into a multi-location retailer with a strong e-commerce presence. Our leadership team focuses on curating the right assortment, supporting local creative communities, and using sales and inventory data to guide buying decisions across stores and channels.

---

## What We Sell

RetailStar sells fine art supplies, craft materials, tools, and project kits across a wide range of creative disciplines. Our assortment includes individual consumables (tube paints, skeins of yarn, sheets of cardstock) as well as starter kits, classroom packs, and seasonal project bundles.

Products span multiple mediums and techniques — painting, drawing, sewing, knitting, crochet, embroidery, paper crafting, jewelry making, clay sculpting, and home décor DIY. Each item is tracked by SKU, category, unit price, and cost price to support margin analysis and inventory planning.

---

## Customer Type

Our primary customers include:

- **Fine artists and illustrators** — buyers of professional-grade paints, brushes, canvas, and drawing materials
- **Fiber artists and sewists** — knitters, crocheters, quilters, and garment makers purchasing yarn, fabric, notions, and patterns
- **Paper crafters and stampers** — scrapbookers, card makers, and journalers shopping for cardstock, patterned paper, stamps, and embellishments
- **Jewelry makers** — hobbyists and small-batch creators buying beads, wire, findings, and pliers
- **Parents and educators** — teachers and caregivers stocking construction paper, safety scissors, pom-poms, and classroom-friendly adhesives
- **Small-business makers** — Etsy sellers, custom gift shops, and artisan vendors purchasing supplies in volume for resale or production
- **Seasonal and event-driven buyers** — customers shopping for holiday wreaths, wedding DIY, party crafts, and gift-making seasons

Many customers are repeat purchasers who return regularly to restock consumables such as acrylic paint, embroidery floss, glue sticks, and yarn.

## Sales Channels

RetailStar operates through multiple sales channels:

| Channel | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Physical stores** | Specialty art & craft locations across multiple regions, each carrying core staples and some localized assortment based on local demand |
| **E-commerce website** | Ship-to-home orders through RetailStar.com |
| **Buy online, pick up in store (BOPIS)** | Online orders fulfilled from the customer's nearest store location |
| **In-store workshops & events** | Make-and-take classes, technique demos, and seasonal craft nights that drive foot traffic and attach sales of related supplies |
| **Seasonal promotions** | Back-to-school, holiday crafting, Mother's Day gifting, and summer camp prep campaigns across online and in-store |

Sales, returns, and inventory are recorded centrally so leadership can compare performance by store, region, category, and channel.

## Business Goals

RetailStar’s leadership team is focused on the following strategic priorities:

1. **Category growth** — Expand sales in high-margin areas such as fine art supplies, premium yarn, and specialty tools
2. **Assortment optimization** — Balance everyday staples (brushes, thread, glue, paper) with trend-driven items (viral craft kits, seasonal décor projects)
3. **Inventory efficiency** — Reduce stockouts on fast-moving SKUs such as white acrylic paint, #8 crochet hooks, and 12×12 cardstock, while avoiding overstock on slow-moving seasonal kits
4. **Return rate reduction** — Identify categories with elevated return rates — often complex kits, specialty tools, or fabric cut-to-order — and address root causes
5. **Community engagement** — Use in-store workshops and maker events to build loyalty and increase basket size on related supplies
6. **Margin protection** — Monitor unit price vs. cost price across categories, especially where supplier costs fluctuate (canvas, yarn, precious-metal jewelry findings)
7. **Omnichannel performance** — Grow online sales of bulk and replenishment items while keeping stores profitable as hands-on creative destinations

Leadership reviews sales, inventory, and return metrics on a weekly and quarterly basis to guide buying, staffing, promotions, and store-level assortment decisions.
